About SERP Nook

SERP Nook is a free meta tag generator and SERP preview tool. Paste your URL, title and description to see exactly how the page will render on Google, Bing and social cards - side by side, with truncation shown the way each engine does it. Character limits are checked as you type, and every tag is one click from your clipboard. No account, no tracking, works offline.

Frequently asked questions

Does this pull live data from Google?

No - and it can't, Google blocks automated SERP requests. Every SERP preview tool simulates the rendering, including this one. Your input never leaves the browser.

Why does Google truncate my title?

Google displays roughly 580 pixels of title, which is about 50-60 characters. Beyond that it cuts with an ellipsis. SERP Nook shows the pixel-limited cut rather than a flat character count.

What's the difference between the social and search preview?

Search results show a title plus description in a small block. Social cards (Facebook, Twitter/X, LinkedIn) use og:title and og:description with different limits, and can include an image. They need to be written separately - SERP Nook previews both.
